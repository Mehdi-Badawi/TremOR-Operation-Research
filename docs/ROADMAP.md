# Roadmap (targets, not commitments)

1. Record labelled taps on cast concrete slabs with built-in delaminations,
   across several phones and hammers.
2. Measure the transparent baseline on that data with grouped splits and
   report abstention rates honestly.
3. Fine-tune a small open audio model (well under 10M parameters) and compare
   it to the baseline.
4. Phone app that produces a per-zone map with a record of every tap.
5. Extend to other materials (tile, plaster, masonry), each with its own
   validation.
6. Publish evaluation protocols and a consented tap-sound dataset.
