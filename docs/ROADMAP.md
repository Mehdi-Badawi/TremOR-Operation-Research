# Roadmap (targets)

## Near term
- **December 2026:** first prototypes to partners (AIS Construction Services,
  Purdue Bowen Laboratory).
- **January 2027:** test kits to Miyamoto International (Kathmandu) and
  Kathmandu University.
- Test specimens and walls at Bowen Laboratory to reproduce results outside
  our Indianapolis lab.

## Models
- Fine-tune a small open audio model (well under 10M parameters) and compare
  it with the transparent baseline.
- Test whether tuning for a specific site or organization improves results;
  organizations can keep private models tuned on their own data.
- Cross-material pretraining, validated for each material.

## Hardware
- Four-sensor taped kit compared against lab equipment.

## 12-month goals
- App and model in use at three or more real sites in the US and Nepal.
- Open dataset of at least 5,000 labelled taps across at least three materials
  and several phone models.
- Published comparison against engineer findings.

## 36-month goals
- Regular use by inspection firms, municipalities and relief groups.
- Private per-organization models; other researchers building on the dataset.

**Measures:** sites and organizations using it, labelled taps contributed,
agreement with engineer findings, abstention rate, repeat use.
